# Auntie Marlene's — Hero Image Nano Banana Prompts

6 cinematic hero scenes for the homepage. Use with **Gemini 2.5 Flash Image**
(`gemini-2.5-flash-image-preview`).

## Workflow

1. **Generate scene 1 first** (The Bonnet Moment). It's the tonal anchor.
2. **Upload a 16:9 landscape reference image** alongside every prompt (otherwise
   Nano Banana defaults to square). You can use any 16:9 photo initially, then
   swap it for scene 1's output once generated.
3. Once scene 1 is approved, **upload it as a style/color reference** for scenes
   2–6 to lock consistent color grading and mood.
4. Generate each scene, review, refine conversationally if needed.
5. Save outputs to `apps/web/public/images/hero/` as `hero-01-bonnet.png` …
   `hero-06-dad-braids.png`.

## Shared style suffix (identical in every prompt — do not change)

> Professional editorial cinematic photograph, shot on 85mm f/1.8 lens with
> shallow depth of field and creamy bokeh, Kodak Portra 400 color grading with
> warm peachy skin tones and rich shadow detail, natural skin texture with
> visible pores and fine detail, magazine-quality production, digital cinema
> camera aesthetic, high-resolution 4K output, 16:9 landscape aspect ratio
> matching the uploaded reference image.

## Shared negative suffix (identical in every prompt)

> Avoiding: illustration, cartoon, painting, 3D render, digital art, stylized or
> anime aesthetic, plastic or waxy skin, overly airbrushed or glossy skin,
> porcelain-smooth perfection, impossible or uniformly diffuse lighting, ambient
> AI glow, extra fingers, missing fingers, distorted hands, anatomically
> incorrect features, stereotypical portrayal, exoticization, objectification,
> culturally insensitive or undignified representation, lightened skin tones,
> inaccurate or synthetic-looking hair texture.

## Final deployment notes

- **Mobile vs desktop:** The ptp pattern uses videos on desktop and static
  images on mobile. For AM v1, use these 6 stills on both, cycling as
  background. If you want video later, use these as keyframes for an
  image-to-video model (Runway, Veo, Kling).
- **Target folder:** `apps/web/public/images/hero/` — named `hero-01-bonnet.png`
  through `hero-06-dad-braids.png`.
- **Optimization:** Run through Next.js `<Image>` with `priority` on index 0.
  Consider AVIF/WebP export at 1920×1080.
- **Hero component update:** Update `apps/web/components/Hero.tsx` to match the
  ptp cycling pattern (`HERO_IMAGES` array + `useEffect` interval + fade
  transitions).
